# Drilldown Roles

Use this file when authoring Slide 2 in `banker_page_pack.json`.
Slide 1 establishes overall industry attractiveness; Slide 2 should drill
into the strongest second-layer structural factor.

Do not hard-code Slide 2 by industry. Select the drilldown based on evidence,
distinctiveness from Slide 1, visualizability, and transaction relevance.

## Selection Logic

1. Identify what Slide 1 already proves.
2. Identify the strongest second-layer insight in the evidence DB.
3. Choose the dimension that best explains growth, profit pool, competitive
   change, or transaction value.
4. Ensure Slide 2 adds a different question, metric set, or structural axis
   rather than repeating Slide 1.

## Candidate Roles

Use one of these roles, or a close variant if the evidence demands it:

- **Market segmentation**: sub-markets, category sizes, growth by segment, or
  profit differences by segment.
- **Customer structure**: customer groups, concentration, retention,
  willingness to pay, or spend per customer.
- **Channel structure**: direct/distributor/platform mix, channel share shift,
  channel economics, or go-to-market change.
- **Application scenarios**: end-use mix, growth by application, emerging use
  cases, or application-led demand shift.
- **Value-chain profit pool**: high-level value distribution and margin pools;
  keep detailed value-chain mechanics for Slide 4.
- **Penetration and benchmark**: adoption rate, mature-market benchmark,
  headroom, or catch-up potential.
- **Technology or product evolution**: product generations, substitution,
  R&D intensity, or architecture change.
- **Policy and regulation**: policy changes that reshape economics, barriers,
  compliance cost, or access.
- **Supply-demand dynamics**: capacity, utilization, pricing, inventory, or
  cycle balance.
- **Competitive structure**: concentration, player types, share shifts, or
  market-structure change; keep detailed peer comparison for Slide 6.

## Pairing Constraints

- Slide 2 must answer a different question from Slide 1.
- Slide 1 and Slide 2 should not rely on the same primary metric set.
- Slide 2 must add at least one new structural information category.
- If the best drilldown cannot be supported with evidence, use a structured
  evidence-boundary exhibit rather than forcing a weak chart.
